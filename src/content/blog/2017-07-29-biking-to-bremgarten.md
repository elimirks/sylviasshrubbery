Today was a beautiful sunny day in Zürich. After a week of rain, I seized the opportunity to bike in the sun! I started biking to Spreitenbach to see the [Bruno Weber Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Weber_Park), which is roughly 10km West of Zürich. However, either fate wanted me to go South, or I'm just bad with directions (admittedly, probably the later) because I ended up heading towards [Bremgarten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremgarten,_Aargau) in the Canton of [Aargau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aargau). I saw a picture of a cool old tower in the Bremgarten Alte Stadt, so I continued to bike there, which was another good option!

As promised in my last post, I took a bunch of cow pictures :)
